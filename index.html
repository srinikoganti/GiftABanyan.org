Why only banyan

India's national tree deserves to come back to our cities

A home for hundreds of lives

Its red figs feed parrots, mynas, barbets, koels and fruit bats through the year. One banyan supports a whole neighbourhood of wildlife.

The deepest shade there is

A mature banyan spreads wide enough to shelter a village gathering. In a Deccan summer, that shade is a gift to every walker, vendor and child.

A tree that outlives all of us

Banyans live for centuries, dropping aerial roots that become new trunks. The tree you plant today will be there for your great-grandchildren.

How it works

Five steps from your gift to a tree that stands on its own

STEP 1

You sponsor or gift

Choose a tree for yourself, or dedicate it to a wedding, birthday, newborn or loved one.

STEP 2

We plant a 2-year-old banyan

In a public park owned by HMDA or a panchayat, with written permission for every site.

STEP 3

A cement guard goes up

A large triangle or square guard keeps goats and cattle away while leaving room to grow.

STEP 4

Two years of slow watering

Two earthen pots buried by the roots are refilled every 10 to 15 days by our own water vehicle.

STEP 5

You watch it grow

Your tree's own page shows its location, watering log, photos and videos from every visit.

Questions

Before you plant

What happens if my tree dies?

We replace it free of charge, at any time during the two years of care. Your tree page will show the replacement date and the new tree's photos.

What happens after two years?

By then the banyan is about four years old and can survive on its own. We keep visiting periodically and uploading photos, so your page stays alive too.

Where are the trees planted?

In open parks and green spaces owned by HMDA or gram panchayats around Hyderabad. We adopt one layout at a time, so our watering vehicle can care for every tree efficiently.

Why plant a 2-year-old tree instead of a seedling?

An older sapling has stronger roots and a thicker stem. Combined with a cement guard and slow pot watering, it has a far better chance of surviving its first summers.

Can I get a tax deduction?

We are a Section 8 non-profit and are applying for 80G registration. Once it's granted, every donation will receive an 80G receipt. Add your PAN when you sponsor so we can issue it.

Can I visit my tree?

Yes. Your tree page has its exact GPS location. It's a public park, so you are welcome any time.
